     OOP WEEK 1 - CALCULATOR DEMO      
========================================

--- Basic Operations ---
10 + 5  = 15.0
10 - 5  = 5.0
10 x 5  = 50.0
10 / 5  = 2.0

--- Edge Case: Division by Zero ---
ERROR: Cannot divide by zero.
10 / 0  = 0.0

--- Enhanced Operations ---
10 % 3  = 1.0

--- Edge Case: Modulus by Zero ---
ERROR: Cannot take modulus by zero.
10 % 0  = 0.0

--- Power ---
2 ^ 10  = 1024.0
5 ^ 0   = 1.0

--- Maximum and Minimum ---
max(25, 75) = 75.0
max(75, 25) = 75.0
min(25, 75) = 25.0
min(75, 25) = 25.0

--- Average (Varargs) ---
average(10,20,30,40,50) = 30.0
average(100, 200)       = 150.0

--- Edge Case: Average with No Arguments ---
ERROR: No numbers provided for average.
average() = 0.0

--- Add Many Numbers (Varargs) ---
addMany(1 to 10) = 55.0
addMany(50,50,50) = 150.0
